How Upkept Works

We’ve designed Upkept to take the uncertainty and friction out of home improvements. Here’s what happens when you use our service:

1. Post your project

Fill out a simple form with details about the job: location, scope, approximate budget and timeline. You’ll also pick three preferred windows for a site visit so contractors can plan accordingly.

2. Vetting & matching

Our team reviews your request and selects two or three vetted contractors. We check licences, insurance, references and even review sample contracts so you don’t have to. Only pros who meet our standards are invited to quote.

3. Receive quotes

You’ll get multiple quotes with transparent pricing and a clear breakdown of milestones. Compare their availability and choose the contractor that best fits your project.

4. Fund escrow (optional)

For extra peace of mind, you can place your project funds into an escrow‑like account. Payments are released only when you confirm each milestone is completed satisfactorily.

5. Complete milestones

Work begins according to the agreed timeline. With each milestone finished, the relevant portion of the funds is released to the contractor. If issues arise, we’re here to help mediate.

6. Review & repeat

After final completion, leave a review for your contractor to help others make informed decisions. Return to Upkept for your next project—we’ll keep improving the process.

Why Upkept?

Vetted Pros

Every contractor undergoes rigorous checks: licences, insurance, references, site visits and contract reviews. No more guessing who to trust.

Escrow‑like Payments

Funds are held in a safe account and released only when work milestones are met. Protect yourself from unfinished work or surprise costs.

Multiple Quotes

Post once and receive up to three quotes from quality pros. See their availability and pick the one that fits your schedule and budget.

How It Works

1. Post a project

Tell us what you need, your timeline and your budget. Give us three preferred dates for site visits, and we’ll do the rest.

2. We verify and match

Our team reviews your request and hand‑picks two or three contractors who meet our vetting standards and fit your scope.

3. Choose and fund

Compare quotes, pick your pro and (optionally) place funds into escrow. Milestone releases mean you only pay as work is completed.

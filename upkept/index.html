Escrow-backed Peace of Mind

UpKept’s escrow flow mirrors best-in-class payment protection: funds are verified, milestones are scheduled, and releases require confirmation from both parties. Built-in lien waivers and contract templates keep your investment safe.

Revenue Model Designed for Growth

  • Hybrid monetization: listing plans plus project commissions.
  • $50/month premium visibility upgrades for contractors.
  • 10% platform fee on completed jobs via escrow.
  • 2% homeowner payment protection per milestone.
  • Forecast: $7,100/month with 30 contractors and 20 jobs.

Trust & Verification Protocol

  • License & insurance confirmation (general liability + WCB).
  • 3–5 reference checks with documented outcomes.
  • Reputation review across BBB and trade associations.
  • Homeowner-visible verification badges for transparency.

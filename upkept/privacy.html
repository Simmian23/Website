Privacy Policy

Effective January 1, 2025

1. Overview

UpKept protects homeowner and contractor information through encryption, strict access controls, and transparent consent. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and store personal data across our platform.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account details such as name, email address, role, and company information.
  • Project data including descriptions, budgets, milestones, and uploaded documents.
  • Verification evidence like licenses, insurance certificates, and reference contact details.
  • Escrow and transaction records required to facilitate milestone-based payments.

3. How We Use Data

We process data to match projects with contractors, manage escrow transactions, enable messaging, and provide support. We do not sell personal information and only share data with trusted service providers that power payments, background checks, or communication tools.

4. Security

All sensitive data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is role-based and audited. Payment details are handled by PCI-compliant providers. We notify impacted users of any security incidents in accordance with Canadian privacy regulations.

5. Your Choices

Users can request data exports or deletion at any time by emailing privacy@upkept.ca. Contractors may update verification documentation through the portal, and homeowners can archive historical projects for their records.

6. Contact

Reach our privacy team at privacy@upkept.ca for questions about this policy or your data rights.

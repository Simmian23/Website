Pricing

We believe in transparent, fair pricing. Here’s how we currently keep the lights on while delivering value to both homeowners and contractors.

Homeowners: Free to Post

Posting a project on Upkept is free. You can receive quotes without any obligation. If you choose to use our escrow service for extra protection, we charge a small percentage fee (displayed before you fund the escrow).

Contractors: Pay on Success

There’s no cost to apply or receive leads. When you win a job through Upkept, a commission is charged on the final project value. This keeps our incentives aligned and means you only pay when you get paid.

Premium Listing (Future)

We plan to offer subscription options for contractors who want additional visibility or bundled escrow fees. Stay tuned as we roll out these features—your feedback will help shape them.
